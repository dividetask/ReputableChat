# The chat's todos

- Limits on who may post in the chat. Today anyone who creates an account
  can post, held only to the chat's own terms (sizes and so on), and the
  reputation system decides who sees what. Wanted later, not yet: for
  example, posting only for accounts somebody trusted has vouched for, or a
  rate limit on new accounts. The agnostic server leaves who may post what
  to the apps.
