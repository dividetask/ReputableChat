ReputableChat rules, version 1

These rules say what every field of every record means and what makes a record
valid. This text is the note of the genesis record. A later version is
published as a new revision of the genesis account's identity declaration, with
the whole new text in its note. Every version stays on the chain.


1. RECORDS

A record is a payload and a signature over it.

The payload is a JSON object in canonical form: keys sorted, no whitespace,
UTF-8, and no floating-point numbers. A number that is not whole is written as
a decimal string, such as "0.5".

The signature is Ed25519 over the canonical bytes of the payload, made by the
key the payload names as its author, written as base64url (64 bytes).

A public key is an Ed25519 public key written as base64url (32 bytes). An
account is its key; nothing else identifies it.

A record hash is SHA-256 over the text "reputablechat:record:v1", a newline,
the canonical payload, a newline, and the signature, written as 64 lowercase
hex characters. It is the record's identity, and every field that names
another record holds one.

Text is UTF-8 with surrounding whitespace removed. It contains no control
characters other than tab, newline and carriage return. Limits are in bytes. Text is always displayed as text, never as
markup.

A decimal is a string: an optional minus sign, a whole part of at most seven
digits with no leading zeros, and optionally a point and one to eighteen
digits. Unless stated otherwise, a decimal lies between -1 and 1.


2. ORDER

A record follows the newest version of these rules that it acknowledges,
directly or through the records it acknowledges. Order on the chain means
acknowledgement and nothing else; the time a record claims plays no part.

A record written for rules not yet published cannot be valid, since it has
nothing through which to acknowledge them. A record written for older rules
that arrives after newer ones are published is refused, and must be signed
again under the newer rules. A record that was valid under the rules it
followed stays valid.


3. FIELDS MANY RECORDS SHARE

purpose: the kind of record and the shape of its payload, written
"reputablechat:<kind>:v1". It is signed, so a signature made for one kind can
never be passed off as another. When the fields of a kind change, its suffix
moves to v2; records of the old shape stay valid.

revision: a whole number counting how many times the author has published this
record. Each publication must be higher than the last. It is not the shape.

ack: the record hashes of the most recent records the author had seen and chose
to acknowledge, sorted, with no duplicates, at most 16. Empty in the genesis
record and nowhere else. It is what makes the records a chain: removing a
record means removing everything that acknowledged it.

note: free text for a person reading the chain, at most 16,000 bytes, or
null. Software never acts on it. An empty note is null.

ts: the time the author says they signed, in whole seconds since
1970-01-01 00:00 UTC. It is the author's own claim, checked against nothing,
and it orders nothing.


4. KINDS OF RECORD

Identity declaration, reputablechat:identity:v1. Who an account is, in its own
words.
  pubkey: the author.
  revision, ack, note, ts: as above.
  handle: the name shown, 1 to 64 bytes. Handles are not unique.
  bio: 0 to 280 bytes.
  icon: an image, named by the SHA-256 of its bytes in hex plus .png, .jpg,
    .gif or .webp; or null.
  master_pubkey, previous_pubkey: reserved for replacing a key; null in this
    version.

Attestation, reputablechat:attestation:v1. What an account thinks of everyone
else.
  pubkey: the author.
  revision, ack, note, ts: as above.
  scores: a map from public key to the author's opinion of that account, at
    most 10,000 entries. Each entry holds:
    reputation: a decimal, the author's rating of that account.
    trust: a decimal, what that account's recommendations are worth to the
      author. It multiplies along a path; 0 stops the path there.
  derived: the author's own calculated reputations, for others to use as the
    furthest term of theirs:
    hops: how far out they reach, 0 to 7.
    params: the SHA-256 in hex of the parameters they were calculated under.
    scores: a map from public key to a decimal, at most 50,000 entries.

Adjustment, reputablechat:adjustment:v1. One change to an attestation, between
publications of it.
  pubkey: the author.
  base_revision: the revision of the author's attestation this amends.
  seq: its place among the adjustments to that revision, from 1. Each seq is
    used once.
  target: the public key whose entry changes.
  reputation, trust: the new values for that entry, as in an attestation.
  ack, note, ts: as above.
  A new attestation replaces every adjustment to the one before it.

Message, reputablechat:message:v1. Text one account sends to a room.
  author: the sender.
  room: 1 to 64 characters of lowercase letters, digits and hyphens, not
    starting with a hyphen. It is signed, so a message cannot be moved to
    another room.
  seq: the author's count of their own messages across every room, from 1.
    Each seq is used once.
  prev: the record hash of the author's previous message, or null for their
    first.
  reply_to: the record hash of the message this answers, or null.
  body: 1 to 4,000 bytes.
  ack, note, ts: as above.

Emote, reputablechat:emote:v1. One account's response to one message.
  author: who responds.
  room: the room of that message.
  message: the record hash of that message.
  emote: one of a closed list published by the server, each counting as
    positive, negative or neutral.
  ack, note, ts: as above.
  One per account per message, and final. An emote also moves the author's
  rating of the message's author, so it is its own adjustment.

Notice, reputablechat:notice:v1. An official statement from a publisher.
  publisher: the author.
  revision: counts every notice the publisher has published.
  kind: one of founding, policy, outage, release or advisory.
  title: 1 to 120 bytes.
  body: 1 to 16,000 bytes.
  supersedes: the record hash of the notice this one corrects, or null. A
    founding notice supersedes nothing. A correction is a new notice; nothing
    is ever edited.
  ack, note, ts: as above.

Release, reputablechat:release:v1. A published version of the client.
  publisher: the author.
  revision, ack, note, ts: as above.
  label: the version's name, 1 to 64 bytes.
  files: a map from path to the SHA-256 in hex of that file's bytes, 1 to 500
    entries. A path is at most 256 bytes and never contains "..".
  notes: 0 to 1,000 bytes.


5. RATING AND REPUTATION

A rating is what one account gives another, in its own attestation: friending,
emoting and reporting move it, and it lies between -1 and 1. A reputation is
what a reader calculates for an account from their own rating of it and the
ratings others have given it. Every reader calculates their own; there is no
reputation that belongs to everyone.
